【崩坏：星穹铁道】星穹列车
一个小型辅助向二次元mod。

[h1]本mod包含以下内容：[/h1]
[olist]
    [*]一个建筑【星穹列车停靠站】。
    [*]一个国际组织【星穹列车】。
    [*]来自星穹的 70+ 名自机角色，以及他们各自所属阵营的纹章。
    [*]一个阶层【命途行者】以及各属性的特权。角色从属于该阶层。
[/olist]

[h2]使用方法[/h2]
[olist]
    [*]在首都建造建筑【星穹列车停靠站】，过月后便会自动加入国际组织【星穹列车】。
[/olist]

[h1]玩法概览[/h1]
[olist]
    [*]在首都建造【星穹列车停靠站】后即可加入国际组织【星穹列车】，每月获得星琼。
    [*]使用星琼可以进行跃迁，招募来自星穹的角色；亦可购买列车补给获得额外星琼。
    [*]角色从属于【命途行者】阶层，授予特权后可以将其雇佣进入内阁、担任将领，甚至成为统治者或外派治理附庸。
    [*]星穹列车停靠站与命途行者能提高探索与殖民能力、开辟新天地。
[/olist]

[Honkai: Star Rail] Astral Express
A small auxiliary-style anime-themed mod.

[h1]This mod includes the following content:[/h1]
[olist]
    [*]A building: [Astral Express Station].
    [*]An international organization: [Astral Express].
    [*]70+ playable characters from the star rail, along with the emblems of their respective factions.
    [*]An estate: [Pathstrider] along with its privileges for various attributes. Characters belong to this estate.
[/olist]

[h2]How to Use[/h2]
[olist]
    [*]Construct the [Astral Express Station] building in your capital. After one month passes, you will automatically join the international organization [Astral Express].
[/olist]

[h1]Gameplay Overview[/h1]
[olist]
    [*]After constructing the [Astral Express Station] in your capital, you join the international organization [Astral Express] and gain Stellar Jade each month.
    [*]Use Stellar Jade to perform Warp and recruit characters from the star rail; you can also purchase the Express Supply Pass for extra Stellar Jade.
    [*]Characters belong to the [Pathstrider] estate. After granting privileges, you can hire them into the cabinet, appoint them as generals, or even make them rulers or send them to govern subject nations.
    [*]The Astral Express Station and Pathstriders improve exploration and colonization capabilities, opening up new frontiers.
[/olist]
